## E. Plug-and-Play Result
A fresh BHIV capability can discover and invoke Insight without custom integration. 
The Insight execution endpoints are formally registered with a public `https` URL in the live BHIV platform registry. Any platform runtime utilizing `PlatformCapabilitySDK.invoke_capability(service_id="insightflow.runtime.intelligence.v1", ...)` will automatically pull the live execution endpoint from discovery and properly format, authenticate, and trace the execution, requiring ZERO custom local setup.
